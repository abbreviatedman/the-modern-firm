# Scars and Scales

### Terminology

- female dragons are called dragonesses
- young dragons are called dragonlings

### Tribes

##### Bubble Tail

- Built for swimming.
- Slow at flying and swimming... more like floaters/gliders.
- Generally peaceful and slow to make decisions.
- Led by a council of queens, like a school of fish that vote on decisions together and debate for what feels to other tribes like _ages_.
- prefer not to even swim at all, riding whales and bubble buses
- their scales are softer to help them swim better
_ queens:gull,plunge,manta,ray,ripple

##### Lava Tail

- live in volcanos
- fireproof
- can live 2 weeks without water
- usually very healthy
- reputation as hot-headed
- good flight abilities
- red, orange, and yellow
- dragonlings are white and develop color at age 4
- have to avoid cold temperatures or they turn slowly to stone
- Queen: Igneous
- 
##### Thunder Tail

- love storms
- great navigation skills
- slow to anger, but given to rages when angered
- lightning can shoot from their talons if angered
- usually black, white, or gray
- most popular name: "Bolt"
- Queen: Flash

##### Moon Tail

- can breathe in the upper atmosphere, or even in space
- mostly nocturnal
- can speak to the moon, which they worship
- pale white coloring is common, but sometimes black dotted with white (like a starry sky)
- excellent night vision
- love playing in the rings around the planet, especially the young
- their palace is in one of the asteroids of the rings
- have never been to the moon, but claim they almost have
- Queen: Meteorite

##### Eagle Tail

- very brave, strong, bold, and maybe overconfident or even cocky
- excellent hunters, big part of their culture
- speak to birds
- are very protective of their young and their friends and their birds
- usually gold or silver, but scales feel like feathers
- spikes on their tails are softer than usual dragons
- Queen: Swoop

##### Heart Tail

- tails have heart-shaped clubs at the end and retractable claws
- are agile warriors
- are red, pink, or deep purple, color changes when excited
- are generally peace-loving, but will kill if attacked
- are considered gracefully beautiful/handsome
- love art and nature and dance
- make paint from moss
- Queen: Violet

##### Serpent Tail

- the strongest warriors
- colors are red, yellow, dark green
- tails have rattles
- are fierce when fighting
- tend to be the first to strike in a battle
- have venom, which is produced from the blood of other Serpent Tails (almost always consensual)
- known for rising up to pounce
- Queen: Cobra

##### Ghost Tail

- ancient tribe that lives in abandoned cities underground
- they've been hiding away because they grew more fearful of outsiders
  - partly because their powers are not helpful in battle
- powers are:
  - short-term invisibility
  - can speak to their own ancestors
  - have wings that capture air and let them float without flapping
- possibly they hid because a princess was killed by an outsider hundreds of years ago
- possibly their queens have kept control of the populace through this fear of outsiders

### Major Characters

##### Gannet 

- a Bubble Tail
- male
- a prince
- mother is Queen Manta, father is King Wave
- light blue with light green underscales
- seems slow and prone to overthinking to his others, but seen as decisive, rash, or impulsive to his own tribe
- quiet and peace-loving, always trying to find a diplomatic solution
- in love with Cormorant

##### Crescent

- a Moon Tail
- female
- mother is Nova, father is Quasar
- an outsider in the tribe
  - her parents raised her while traveling among the tribes
  - they believe that other tribes are worth exploring
  - this is how she meets the other characters, wandering on her own through the tribes' lands
- does not want to ever get married or have dragonets, feels there's so much more to explore
  - meets Galaxy, who changes her mind
- is black with white dots

### Minor Characters

##### Galaxy

- is a Moon Tail
- pale black with some mixed grey and white
- stumbled upon Crescent when she was hiking
- ran away from an orphanage and was living in the wild when she met Crescent
- fascinated by science and magic, is always experimenting (once blew up a star)
- in love with Crescent

- is playful and funny

- she's fiercely protective with her pets, family, and Gannet
